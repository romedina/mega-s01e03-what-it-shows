  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Day-first tenants get day and month swapped: "7 April went out on 4 July". This is the fix that is queued.
  • hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, and reads the date the tenant's way, day first. People type what the hint tells them, so form bookings at booking-v3 tenants swap even after the parser is fixed. A different bug, in the form.
  • shift: the date moves by one day, not by a swap. "Tuesday 12 May, calendar says Monday 11 May." "Client in Lisbon got Tuesday, client in Toronto got Monday." Time zones, not date order. The desk saw these (desk-notes.json) and still filed them under parser.

Why it merged

The desk merges on subject line alone (decision 0001). Twelve subject lines ("Wrong day on the invite", "Date is wrong", …) cover all three problems, so every report landed in #4471. The intake tag did not help either: it guesses from the reporter's browser, and the table shows how often it disagrees with the tenant.
