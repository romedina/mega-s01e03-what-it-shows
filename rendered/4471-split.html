5 reports read as a one-day move; filed under parser by tenant.

The reports read as a one-day move stay counted under parser, where the desk filed them by tenant, and are footnoted under the table. They are a different problem: "Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May." "Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday." A day/month fix cannot move a date by one day, so the parser fix does not close them.

Why it merged

The desk merges on subject line alone (decision 0001). The same subject lines ("Wrong day on the invite", "Date is wrong", …) came in for all three problems, so every report landed in #4471. The intake tag guesses from the reporter's browser; the "tagged as" column shows how often it disagrees with the tenant.

Since April

Lessons

  • Closed is not done: check every problem a ticket holds before you close it.
  • A date that moves by one day is not a date that swaps. Read the body, not the subject.
